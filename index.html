Design Philosophy
01
User-Centred First
Every design decision starts with the person using it. I research, test, and iterate to ensure the experience feels intuitive not just visually polished.
02
Consistency Through Systems
I believe in building scalable UI foundations organised components, maintained libraries, and clear hierarchies that make entire products feel coherent.
03
Design & Dev in Sync
Great design lives in the product, not just the file. I work closely alongside developers to ensure designs translate accurately into the final experience.
